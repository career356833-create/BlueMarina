# Blue Marina V1 — Next Roadmap

**Next primary priority: verified Charter supply and inquiry launch.** The existing informational fishing journey has real data, while the Charter entry has zero offers. The next work should create a complete, trustworthy user outcome rather than add a species, one source or one button. This order is by user and business value; dependencies are called out instead of being mistaken for completed work. See the [master status](BLUE_MARINA_V1_MASTER_STATUS.md).

| Order | Initiative | Why now / user value | Business value | Dependencies | Effort | Risk |
| ---: | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Verified Charter supply and inquiry launch** | A user who researched a spot needs a real, attributable trip offer and a clear inquiry path. Zero offers make the current Charter surface an empty shell. | First measurable supply-to-inquiry conversion and operator relationship. | Authorized operator agreements; exact offer/price/port evidence; Auth/admin review; Charter migration and staging RLS E2E; honest availability and no false booking confirmation. | LARGE | HIGH |
| 2 | **Identity and trust foundation** | Saved spots, account continuity, operator submissions, market review and private Operations need verified actors. | Enables repeat use and governed supply instead of local-only sessions. | Supabase environment identification and explicit migration approval; Auth roles and negative tests; Charter/Market/Account RLS E2E; Operations admin actor; no feature-flag shortcut. | LARGE | HIGH |
| 3 | **Real-device Navigation and PWA field gate** | A browser emulator cannot validate GPS, compass, pinch gestures, background resume or installed offline behavior on water. | Protects trust in the core fishing journey and defines the honest Navigation Beta boundary. | Android/iPhone test devices, field protocol, permission-deny/reallow and location privacy checks; retain straight-line disclaimer and four coordinate holds. | MEDIUM | HIGH |
| 4 | **Dependable Today Sea source package** | RISA works, but a user's sea picture remains partial with FEMO and KMA/KHOA held. | Improves utility and repeat visits without a fabricated safety verdict. | Source licenses/keys, source-time freshness, quota and latency budgets, cold-request tests, explicit station/zone choices, independent failure cards and rollback plan. | LARGE | HIGH |
| 5 | **Moderated Market and Community supply pilot** | Empty listings/posts cannot create exchange or participation; local drafts are not public outcomes. | Secondary commerce and retention loops once verified supply and governance exist. | Identity/trust foundation, seller/poster onboarding, image storage contract, human moderation, sparse pilot data and privacy safeguards; payment/chat remain separate decisions. | LARGE | HIGH |

The first initiative is a product milestone, not permission to bulk-scrape restricted booking sites or infer price, seat, schedule, departure coordinate or availability. A credible pilot with a small set of consenting operators and verified offers is more valuable than another large unverified inventory. Each initiative should have a complete user-flow and measurable acceptance gate before moving to the next.
