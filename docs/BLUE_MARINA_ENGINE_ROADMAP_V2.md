# Blue Marina Engine Roadmap V2

Moderation is now an operational loop for private submissions. The next work should convert verified supply into useful public content without weakening the approval boundary.

1. **Charter supply and publication:** obtain explicit provider consent and confirm operator, boat, departure, offer and contact evidence. Review a promotion candidate separately before creating any public offer. Keep availability, seats and schedules unknown until confirmed.
2. **Moderation operations:** complete the Kakao `/community/new` browser return check and authenticated admin visual QA at 1280×900 and 390×844. Give staff a documented role-assignment and revocation procedure. Keep `operations_admin` limited to counts unless a domain role is explicitly granted.
3. **Market supply:** repeat the private image-staging and approved-image exposure E2E with QA assets; ensure a rejected or hidden item never exposes images or seller contact through public APIs.
4. **Community trust:** verify a real second-user report and owner notification/status copy. Keep report decisions and post decisions separate; retain immutable actor/state history.
5. **Platform navigation:** revisit `/boatpedia`, `/coming-soon`, `/dictionary` and `/marine-knowledge` only when a concrete inbound or next-action route is specified. Avoid a broad redesign merely to reduce an audit count.

The seven core journeys remain connected with the documented friction. AIS, recommendations, payments and new notification infrastructure are not dependencies of this moderation release.
